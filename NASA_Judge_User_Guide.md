## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to the NASA Teams Judging System! This guide will help you navigate the system and complete your judging responsibilities effectively.

### What You'll Be Doing

- **Stage 1**: Evaluate teams on general criteria (Innovation, Technical Implementation, Impact, Presentation, Overall Quality)
- **Stage 2**: Evaluate qualified teams on award-specific criteria
- **Scoring**: Rate teams on a 1-5 scale for each criterion
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your scoring progress

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection
- Your judge credentials (provided by admin)

### Scoring Interface

**Screenshot Placeholder**: _[Insert screenshot of scoring form]_

#### Scoring Criteria (1-5 Scale)

1. **Innovation** (1-5)
   - 1: No innovation, basic approach
   - 2: Minimal innovation, slight improvement
   - 3: Moderate innovation, some new elements
   - 4: Good innovation, creative solutions
   - 5: Exceptional innovation, groundbreaking approach

2. **Technical Implementation** (1-5)
   - 1: Poor technical execution, major flaws
   - 2: Below average, some technical issues
   - 3: Average technical quality
   - 4: Good technical execution, minor issues
   - 5: Excellent technical implementation

3. **Impact** (1-5)
   - 1: No clear impact or benefit
   - 2: Minimal impact, limited scope
   - 3: Moderate impact, some benefit
   - 4: Good impact, clear benefits
   - 5: Significant impact, transformative potential

4. **Presentation** (1-5)
   - 1: Poor presentation, unclear communication
   - 2: Below average presentation
   - 3: Average presentation quality
   - 4: Good presentation, clear communication
   - 5: Excellent presentation, compelling delivery

5. **Overall Quality** (1-5)
   - 1: Poor overall quality
   - 2: Below average quality
   - 3: Average quality
   - 4: Good quality
   - 5: Exceptional quality

## 📝 Scoring Guidelines

### General Scoring Principles

#### Be Consistent

- Use the same standards throughout your judging
- Reference your previous scores for similar quality work
- Maintain objectivity across all teams

#### Be Thorough

- Read all provided team information
- Consider all aspects of each criterion
- Take time to make thoughtful evaluations

#### Be Fair

- Judge based on merit, not personal preferences
- Consider the challenge constraints
- Account for different approaches and styles

### Scoring Tips

#### Before You Start

1. **Familiarize Yourself**: Review all teams in your panel first
2. **Set Standards**: Establish your baseline for each score level
3. **Plan Your Time**: Allocate sufficient time for each team

#### During Scoring

1. **Read Carefully**: Review all team materials thoroughly
2. **Score Independently**: Evaluate each criterion separately
3. **Document Reasoning**: Use comments to explain your scores
4. **Take Breaks**: Avoid fatigue that might affect consistency

#### After Scoring

1. **Review Your Scores**: Check for consistency and completeness
2. **Verify Submission**: Ensure all teams are scored and submitted
3. **Report Issues**: Contact admin if you encounter problems

### Common Scoring Scenarios

#### High-Quality Teams (4-5 Range)

- Clear innovation and creativity
- Strong technical execution
- Well-defined impact and benefits
- Professional presentation
- Comprehensive solution

#### Average Teams (3 Range)

- Some good elements but lacking in others
- Adequate technical implementation
- Moderate impact potential
- Decent presentation
- Standard approach

#### Below-Average Teams (1-2 Range)

- Limited innovation or creativity
- Technical issues or poor execution
- Unclear impact or benefits
- Poor presentation quality
- Incomplete or unclear solution
